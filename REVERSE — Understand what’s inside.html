About

Understand what’s inside.

REVERSE is an educational product-anatomy platform. Component information here is general educational content, not model-specific documentation. Nothing here is an instruction to dismantle hazardous devices. Thirty-two products are included, each with its own multi-part 3D model built from simple shapes, plus general educational data.

Why it matters

Many people throw away objects without knowing what is inside or whether one small part failed. REVERSE makes that structure visible so a person can ask better questions, try safe checks, and judge repair versus replacement. It supports the right-to-repair idea of understanding the things you own.

How it is built

Interactive 3D (Three.js) with every part its own mesh; a structured component, fault and repairability database; a relationship engine; an AI layer constrained to that database; and a rule-based fallback. Next: more products, part dragging, community-verified documentation.
